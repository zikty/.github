# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in zikty projects a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior: being respectful, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback, focusing on what's best for the project.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, publishing
others' private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject contributions and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
